01 · Accounting & Payroll
ProductionLaravel 13 · MySQL · BladeTailwind 4 · Vite

Accounting & Payroll System

The payroll platform for a three-company group. It manages each pay period from creation to posted payslips, and it works directly with a shared HR database that other systems also depend on.

  • Calculation engine covering overnight shifts, overtime, night differential, regular and special holidays, and salary changes that take effect mid-period.
  • Rated, Fixed and Daily salary types, each with its own payslip.
  • Payroll editing with day-level pay overrides, recurring and one-time add-ons and deductions, and a full before-and-after audit history.
  • Dashboard of net pay by company and bank, with encrypted bank details.
  • Sign-in with Google Authenticator codes and session-based access control.
  • HR reports for attendance, leave credits, benefits and infractions.

02 · Employee Portal
ProductionLaravel 13 · MySQL · BladeTailwind 4 · Vite

Employee Self-Service Portal

One place for employees and approvers to handle daily HR work, replacing the pages of an older system one module at a time.

  • Attendance clock with a desktop-only rule, attendance calendar and view-only payslips protected by a PIN.
  • Leave, overtime, time adjustment and other applications, with approval queues scoped to each approver.
  • Company memos with read tracking, handbook, infractions, trackers with CSV export, and site transfers.
  • Rate limiting per employee and route, because a whole office shares one IP address.
  • Notification inbox, announcements and four interface themes.
Live demo
03 · Admin Console
ProductionLaravel 13 · MySQL · BladeTailwind 4

Admin Console

A separate control center where administrators manage who can do what across the other apps, without a code change.

  • Page permissions by designation, department or employee, replacing rules that were hard-coded in the portal, with a history of every change.
  • Portal user management with temporary password resets and account locks.
  • Login monitoring that shows who is online, sign-in history and failed attempts.
  • Company, department, job title and offense setup that protects records already in use.
04 · Legacy Migration
ProductionLegacy PHP → LaravelShared MySQL · Linux VPS

Legacy HR System Migration

The work that ties these projects together. A single legacy PHP application was split into standalone Laravel apps with their own sign-in, while the old system stayed live for daily use.

  • Every app shares one HR database, so schema changes are checked for their effect on all of them.
  • New features are built against a separate copy of the database before reaching live data.
  • Hosting, deployment and site setup handled on a Linux VPS with CloudPanel.
05 · Inventory
ProductionLaravel

Inventory System

The final module in the migration. It moves inventory out of the legacy platform and into its own app, following the same structure as the others.
